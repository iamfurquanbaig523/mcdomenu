A McDonald's Hamburger costs $2.49 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.99 in Mississippi to $3.49 in Hawaii. It has just 250 calories — the lowest-calorie burger on the entire McDonald's menu and one of the cheapest items you can order.

Hamburger Price by State (2026)

What's in a McDonald's Hamburger?

One 100% beef patty (1.6 oz before cooking), pickles, diced onions, ketchup, and mustard on a plain toasted bun. No cheese, no lettuce, no special sauce — just the basics. The patty is the same one used in the Cheeseburger, McDouble, and Double Cheeseburger. The Hamburger has been on the McDonald's menu since the first restaurant opened in 1955.

Nutrition Facts

At 250 calories and 9g fat, the Hamburger has roughly half the calories of a Big Mac (590 cal) and a third of the fat of a Double Quarter Pounder (42g fat). If you are counting calories but still want a real burger, this is your best option on the menu.

Hamburger vs Other Value Burgers

The Hamburger is the cheapest at $2.49 but also has the least protein at 12g. Adding cheese ($0.30 more for a Cheeseburger) gives you 3g more protein and 50 more calories. The best value for protein is the McDouble — just $0.40 more than the Hamburger but double the beef and 22g of protein.

Budget Meal Combos

A Hamburger ($2.49) with Small Fries ($1.59) and water comes to $4.08 — the cheapest burger meal you can build at McDonald's. The Hamburger is also part of the $5 Meal Deal alongside a 4-piece McNuggets, small fries, and small drink. The Hamburger Happy Meal at about $5.89 includes fries or apple slices, a drink, and a toy.

Tips and Deals

The McDonald's app runs Buy One Add One for $1 deals where you can add a second Hamburger for just $1. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward free burgers. If you want more food for not much more money, upgrading to a McDouble for $0.40 gives you double the beef — the best value upgrade on the menu.
