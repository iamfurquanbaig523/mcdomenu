A Spicy McCrispy costs $4.99 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $4.29 in Mississippi to $6.49 in Hawaii. It has 470 calories — the same calories as the regular McCrispy but with cayenne pepper heat in the breading, making it a free flavor upgrade.

What's in a Spicy McCrispy?

A whole chicken breast fillet coated in spicy pepper-seasoned breading, served with crinkle-cut pickles on a toasted potato roll. The spice level is moderate — a slow-building warmth from cayenne pepper, not an immediate burn. The chicken is the same whole breast fillet used in the regular McCrispy, just with the spicy breading. If you find the regular McCrispy too plain, the spicy version adds real flavor at the same price.

Nutrition Facts

The Spicy McCrispy has identical calories to the regular McCrispy (470). Sodium is slightly higher at 1,280mg versus 1,140mg due to the pepper seasoning. Allergens include wheat, milk, and soy. See our allergen guide.

All Four McCrispy Sandwiches Compared

The Spicy McCrispy is actually the cheapest McCrispy option at some locations. Since the regular and spicy versions have the same calories, choosing spicy is a free flavor upgrade if you enjoy any level of heat. The Deluxe versions add $0.80-$1.30 for lettuce, tomato, and mayo — 60 extra calories.

Spicy McCrispy vs Competitors

The Spicy McCrispy competes directly with Chick-fil-A's Spicy Sandwich ($5.89, 450 cal), Popeyes Spicy Chicken ($5.49, 700 cal), and Wendy's Spicy Chicken ($5.99, 490 cal). McDonald's version has fewer calories than all except Chick-fil-A. The McCrispy wins on availability with 13,500 US locations versus Chick-fil-A's 3,000.

Meal and Deals

The Spicy McCrispy Meal with medium fries and a medium drink costs about $9.49-$10.49. For a budget alternative, the McChicken at $2.29 is less than half the price. The McDonald's app runs McCrispy BOGO deals frequently. Check the McValue menu for other chicken value options.
