What's in an Oreo McFlurry?

McDonald's vanilla soft-serve ice cream blended with crushed Oreo cookie pieces. The cookies are mixed using the McFlurry spoon, which doubles as a mixing tool that attaches to the machine. The result is a thick, creamy dessert with chunks of chocolate cookie throughout. The Oreo pieces add a satisfying crunch and chocolate-cream flavor that pairs perfectly with the vanilla base.

Nutrition Facts

At 510 calories and 64g of sugar, the Oreo McFlurry is one of the highest-calorie desserts on the menu. A mini Oreo McFlurry is available on the McValue menu at a smaller portion and lower price if you want the taste without the full calorie load.

Oreo McFlurry vs Other Desserts

The Oreo McFlurry costs more than double the price of a Hot Fudge Sundae and has 180 more calories. If you want chocolate flavor without the high calorie count, the sundae at $2.89 is the better value. The M&M's McFlurry has 130 more calories than the Oreo version — the Oreo is actually the lighter McFlurry option.

McFlurry Flavors Available

Oreo and M&M's are the two permanent McFlurry flavors. Limited-time flavors rotate throughout the year — recent options have included the Derpy McFlurry with Berry Popping Pearls. Mini versions of both permanent flavors are available on the McValue menu at a lower price point.

Ice Cream Machine and Deals

The McFlurry depends on the soft-serve machine, which has a reputation for being down. Check the McDonald's app before making a special trip — it shows ice cream availability at your nearest location. Best chance is during regular daytime hours. The McDonald's app occasionally offers dessert promotions including discounted McFlurries.
