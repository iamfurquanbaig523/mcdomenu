What's in a Deluxe McCrispy?

A whole white-meat chicken breast fillet, pressure-cooked in a crispy breading, topped with shredded lettuce, Roma tomato slices, and mayonnaise on a toasted potato roll. The chicken is the same as the regular McCrispy — a single whole breast, not a processed patty. The Deluxe adds the fresh toppings that the regular version skips.

All Four McCrispy Sandwiches Compared

The Deluxe adds $0.80 over the regular McCrispy for lettuce, tomato, mayo, and 6g more protein. The extra toppings make it feel like a more complete sandwich. If you want both fresh toppings and heat, the Spicy Deluxe is the same price.

Meal and Deals

The Deluxe McCrispy Meal with medium fries and drink costs about $10.29-$11.29. The McDonald's app runs McCrispy BOGO deals frequently. For a budget chicken sandwich, the McChicken at $2.29 is less than half the price.
