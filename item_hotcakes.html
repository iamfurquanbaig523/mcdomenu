What Are McDonald's Hotcakes?

Three fluffy pancakes served with a pad of whipped butter and a container of warm maple-flavored hotcake syrup. The batter includes buttermilk for a slightly tangy flavor. They come in a styrofoam container that keeps them warm. McDonald's has served Hotcakes since the breakfast menu launched in 1977 and they remain one of the few sweet breakfast options available.

Nutrition Facts

The 580-calorie count includes butter and syrup. Without them, the three pancakes alone have about 350 calories. Most calories come from carbohydrates — 101g, which is high. Protein is low at 9g, so Hotcakes will not keep you full as long as a sandwich like the Egg McMuffin (310 cal, 17g protein).

Hotcakes vs Other Breakfast Options

If you want pancakes with more protein, the Hotcakes and Sausage ($5.49) adds a sausage patty for 12g more protein. The Fruit & Maple Oatmeal at $3.79 is a lighter sweet option at 320 calories. The Big Breakfast with Hotcakes ($7.49) is the largest breakfast platter — hotcakes, scrambled eggs, sausage, biscuit, and hash browns for 1,340 calories.

Availability and Deals

Hotcakes are only available during breakfast hours — typically until 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends. The $4 Breakfast Meal Deal on the McValue menu sometimes includes Hotcakes as a qualifying item. Check the McDonald's app for breakfast promotions.
