What's in a Sausage McMuffin with Egg?

A pork sausage patty, a freshly-cracked round egg cooked in a ring mold, and a slice of American cheese on a toasted English muffin. The sausage patty has a savory, slightly peppery flavor. This sandwich combines the sausage from the Sausage McMuffin with the egg from the Egg McMuffin, giving you both proteins in one sandwich.

Nutrition Facts

Sausage McMuffin with Egg vs Other Breakfast Sandwiches

The Sausage McMuffin with Egg sits in the middle of the breakfast lineup — more filling than the Egg McMuffin (310 cal) but lighter than the McGriddles (550 cal). If budget matters, the plain Sausage McMuffin at $2.29 saves you $2.70 and still has 14g protein. The Egg McMuffin at $4.49 is the lighter, lower-fat option if you prefer Canadian bacon over sausage.

McMuffin vs McGriddles vs Biscuit

McDonald's serves the sausage-egg-cheese combo on three different breads. The McMuffin uses a toasted English muffin — lightest at 480 calories. The McGriddles uses maple-flavored griddle cakes — 550 calories with a sweeter taste. The Sausage Biscuit with Egg uses a buttermilk biscuit — about 530 calories with a flaky, buttery texture. The McMuffin is the lowest-calorie choice and the most popular of the three.

Meal and Deals

The Sausage McMuffin with Egg Meal adds hash browns and a small coffee for about $6.99-$7.49. The better deal is the $4 Breakfast Meal Deal from the McValue menu — a breakfast sandwich, hash brown, and small coffee for $4 flat. The McDonald's app runs Buy One Add One for $1 breakfast promotions regularly.

Availability

Available during breakfast hours only — typically until 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends. Not on the all-day menu. If you arrive at 10:25 AM, order through the app ahead of time to guarantee you get it before the kitchen switches to lunch.
