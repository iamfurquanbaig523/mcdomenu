What's in Chicken McNuggets?

McNuggets are made from white meat chicken — breast, rib, and tenderloin — cut into pieces, seasoned with a mild spice blend, coated in a light tempura batter, and fried until golden. Each nugget comes in one of four shapes that McDonald's calls the bell, boot, ball, and bone. The shapes are designed to cook evenly and provide a consistent crunch. You get two dipping sauces with a 10-piece order.

Nutrition Facts

Each dipping sauce adds calories: Tangy BBQ adds 45 cal, Sweet 'N Sour adds 50 cal, Honey Mustard adds 60 cal, and Creamy Ranch adds 110 cal. Pick your sauce based on how many extra calories you want.

McNuggets Size Comparison

The 10-Piece costs $0.55 per nugget. If you are ordering for yourself, it is a good balance of value and portion size. The 20-Piece at $0.45 per nugget is better value if you are sharing or want leftovers. The 40-Piece at $0.32 per nugget is the best deal for groups — you save 42% per nugget versus buying four 10-piece boxes.

10-Piece McNuggets Meal

The 10-Piece McNuggets Meal with medium fries and a medium drink costs about $9.49-$10.49. That adds about $4.00-$5.00 for the fries and drink compared to the nuggets alone. If you want to save money, order the 10-piece by itself with a Small Fries ($2.79) — that comes to about $8.28 total versus $9.49+ for the meal combo.

Deals and Tips

The McDonald's app regularly offers free 6-piece McNuggets with a minimum purchase — one of the most common app deals. The 4-piece McNuggets is included in the $5 Meal Deal. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward free McNuggets. McNuggets reheat well in an air fryer at 375°F for 3-4 minutes if you have leftovers from a larger order.
