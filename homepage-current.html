McDonald’s Menu Prices UK 2026 – Full Guide

Welcome to the most comprehensive McDonald’s menu prices UK 2026 guide available online. Whether you’re trying to budget your next visit, check the calorie count of your favourite item, or just curious about the full McDonald’s UK price list, you’re in the right place.

This independent price guide covers every item currently on the McDonald’s UK menu, including burgers, breakfast, drinks, desserts, and sides. We update our prices regularly to ensure accuracy.

How Much Does McDonald’s Cost in the UK in 2026?

McDonald’s UK prices have seen notable increases since 2022, largely driven by inflation, rising ingredient costs, and minimum wage increases. In 2026, you can expect to pay between £1.19 for a hash brown and up to £9+ for a large meal.

The average McDonald’s meal in the UK now costs between £6.99 and £9.49 depending on the item you choose. A Big Mac Meal, the UK’s most popular order, is currently priced at £7.69.

McDonald’s UK Price Increases – What’s Changed?

Between 2022 and 2026, McDonald’s UK raised prices on most items by 20-35%. The biggest increases were seen on beverages and sides, while core burgers like the cheeseburger and hamburger remained relatively affordable at under £2.50.

McDonald’s UK Calories Guide

Since 2022, UK law requires large restaurant chains to display calorie information. McDonald’s now shows calories clearly across its menu, and this guide keeps the most important numbers together with the prices.

Highest-Calorie Items on the McDonald’s UK Menu

The Double Quarter Pounder and Big Breakfast remain among the most calorie-dense items on the menu. If you’re looking for lighter choices, breakfast muffins, burgers without cheese, and selected drinks are safer picks.

McDonald’s UK vs European Prices

UK menu prices usually sit between mainland Europe and Ireland. Airport restaurants, motorway service stations, and central London branches also tend to cost slightly more than standard high street locations.

How to Save Money at McDonald’s UK

The best way to save is to use the official McDonald’s app. App-exclusive deals regularly undercut in-store prices, and MyMcDonald’s Rewards helps you collect points for free food.

McDonald’s UK Saver Menu

Entry-level burgers, selected sides, and breakfast basics still offer the best low-cost value. Pairing saver items with app offers is usually the cheapest way to build a meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about McDonald’s UK menu prices and deals.

How much is a Big Mac in the UK in 2026?+
A Big Mac currently costs £5.29 on its own, or £7.69 as part of a medium meal with fries and a drink. Prices may vary slightly by location.
What time does McDonald’s breakfast end?+
McDonald’s breakfast is served until 11:00am at most UK locations. Some 24-hour restaurants and drive-throughs may offer extended breakfast hours.
Is this site affiliated with McDonald’s?+
No. This is an independent, unofficial price guide. We are not affiliated with McDonald’s Corporation or McDonald’s UK. All prices are sourced from public menus and may vary by location.
How often are prices updated?+
We review and update prices monthly. Our last full update was April 2026. If you spot an error, use your contact workflow to let readers know.
How can I save money at McDonald’s?+
The best way is to use the McDonald’s app, which offers regular exclusive deals. MyMcDonald’s Rewards also lets you earn points for free food.
