⚠️ Hours can vary by franchise and 24-hour status. Always verify your exact restaurant hours in the official McDonald’s app before ordering.

📌 Quick Tips
📱 Use the McDonald’s app to confirm live breakfast cut-off times at your local restaurant.
🚗 Drive-thru restaurants often have the most predictable breakfast-to-lunch transition flow.
⏰ Monday through Friday breakfast typically ends earlier than on Saturday or Sunday.
🍴 Bagels, platters, and hash browns are breakfast-only in the current USA menu coverage and disappear after the breakfast window.
🍟 Lunch and dinner menu availability is usually strongest once the breakfast menu clears, especially for burgers and fries.

McDonald's USA History & Locations

1955
McDonald's National Expansion Begins
Ray Kroc opened the first McDonald's restaurant for the modern system in Des Plaines, Illinois, launching the chain's coast-to-coast expansion.
1979
Happy Meal Debuts
The Happy Meal became a defining kids' format and remains one of the most recognizable quick-service meal bundles in the US.
1983
Chicken McNuggets Go Mainstream
McNuggets became a lasting part of the US menu and eventually grew into one of the brand's biggest group-order and snack categories.
2000s
McCafe Growth Accelerates
McCafe helped McDonald's expand from burgers and fries into coffeehouse-style drinks, iced coffees, lattes, and frappes.
2026
McValue, BIG ARCH and Limited-Time Meals
The current USA menu file highlights McValue savings, BIG ARCH, Snack Wraps, seasonal promo meals, and limited-time desserts as major traffic drivers.

McDonald's USA Menu Prices - Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a Big Mac in the USA?+

The attached USA menu data lists the Big Mac at $5.99 on the core burger menu. Local restaurant, app, tax, and delivery pricing can still change the final total.

What is on the McValue menu right now?+

The current McValue lineup includes $5 McChicken and McDouble meal deals, an about-$6 Daily Double meal deal, breakfast buy one add one for $1 items like Sausage Biscuit and Hash Browns, lunch and dinner add-on picks like McChicken, Double Cheeseburger, 6 pc McNuggets, Small Fries, plus mini McFlurry treats.

How much is a Happy Meal in the USA?+

This USA update lists Hamburger Happy Meal at about $5.89, 4 pc McNuggets Happy Meal at about $6.19, and 6 pc McNuggets Happy Meal at about $7.29.

What time does McDonald's serve breakfast in the USA?+

The attached breakfast data notes that breakfast is typically served until 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends, although exact cutoffs can vary by restaurant.

How many calories are in McDonald's large fries?+

Large World Famous Fries are listed at 480 calories in the current USA menu data used on this site.

Does McDonald's USA have a vegan burger?+

The attached USA menu file does not show a national vegan burger. The current lineup is focused on burgers, chicken, breakfast, fries, desserts, drinks, and coffee.

How much is a McFlurry in the USA?+

The current USA sweets data lists a regular OREO McFlurry at $5.59, a regular M&M's McFlurry at $5.59, and mini McFlurry options at $3.19 in the McValue section.

What is the cheapest item on the McDonald's USA menu?+

The lowest paid items in the attached USA menu data are Vanilla Cone at $1.29 and the Honest Kids Appley Ever After juice box at $1.29, followed by several $1.69 drink options.
