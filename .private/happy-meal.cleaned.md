# McDonald's Happy Meal Menu: Prices, Calories & Kids Meal Guide (USA 2026)

McDonald's Happy Meal menu is the most recognized kids meal in the USA - and it has been since 1979. Three tracked options, a choice of side, a kid-friendly drink, and a toy, all bundled into one price that ranges from $5.89 for the Hamburger Happy Meal up to $7.29 for the 6-pc McNuggets Happy Meal.

## How the McDonald's Happy Meal Menu Works

Every McDonald's Happy Meal follows the same bundle structure regardless of which entree you choose:

-   **Entree:** Hamburger, 4-pc Chicken McNuggets, or 6-pc Chicken McNuggets
    
-   **Side:** [Apple Slices](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/fries-sides/apple-slices/) or kid-sized [World Famous Fries](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/fries-sides/world-famous-fries-small/) (your choice)
    
-   **Drink:** [1% Low Fat Milk Jug](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/1-low-fat-milk-jug/), [Reduced Sugar Low Fat Chocolate Milk](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/reduced-sugar-low-fat-chocolate-milk-jug/), [DASANI Water](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/dasani-water-bottle/), or [Honest Kids Juice Box](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/honest-kids-appley-ever-after-juice-box/)
    
-   **Toy:** One Happy Meal toy (changes with current promotion)
    

**The 600-calorie cap:** McDonald's keeps every Happy Meal on US menu boards at 600 calories or less. Kid-sized portions, fruit as a side option, and low-calorie drink choices all work together to hold totals in check.

**Availability:** Happy Meals are available during lunch and dinner hours. Breakfast Happy Meals are not standard in the USA. They typically become available after breakfast service ends, around 10:30 AM on weekdays. Check [McDonald's breakfast hours](https://mcdomenuusa.com/breakfast-hours/) for timing at your location.

> **Price disclaimer:** All prices on this page reflect current tracked USA menu data. Your local McDonald's may charge more or less depending on franchise, location, taxes, and active promotions.

## Hamburger Happy Meal

The [Hamburger Happy Meal](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/happy-meal/hamburger-happy-meal-w-apple-slices-or-sm-fries-drink-toy/) is the entry-level option on the McDonald's Happy Meal menu and the cheapest tracked kids meal at approximately $5.89. The entree is the same [McDonald's Hamburger](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/burgers-menu/hamburger/) served across the rest of the menu - 100% beef patty, pickles, ketchup, mustard, and onions on a toasted bun, scaled to a kids meal price.

### What's Included

| Component | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Entree | McDonald's Hamburger (100% beef patty, pickles, ketchup, mustard, onions) |
| Side | Apple Slices or kid-sized World Famous Fries |
| Drink | Choice of 1% milk, chocolate milk, water, or juice box |
| Toy | One Happy Meal toy (promotion-dependent) |
| Calories | ~475 kcal (standard build with milk and Apple Slices) |
| Price | ~$5.89 |

### Who It's For

The Hamburger Happy Meal is the right call for kids who prefer beef over chicken, for parents looking for the lowest-priced Happy Meal option, or for younger children who find the hamburger format easier to eat than nuggets.

**Calorie note:** At ~475 kcal, the Hamburger Happy Meal is actually heavier than the 4-pc McNuggets Happy Meal (~420 kcal). If calorie count is the priority, the 4-pc McNuggets version is the lighter choice despite being a chicken option.

For allergen detail on the hamburger patty and bun, the [nutrition and allergen guide](https://mcdomenuusa.com/mcdonalds-nutrition-calories-allergens/) has the full breakdown including wheat, dairy, and cross-contact information.

**Value comparison:** At $6.19, it costs $0.30 more than the Hamburger Happy Meal but delivers 55 fewer calories and a chicken option. For parents who want the lightest meal at a reasonable price, this is the strongest pick on the McDonald's Happy Meal menu.

## Side and Drink Options Inside the McDonald's Happy Meal Menu

Every Happy Meal lets you choose the side and drink independently of the entree. These choices have a meaningful impact on the final calorie total.

### Apple Slices vs. Kid-Sized Fries

| Side | Calories | Notes |

| [Apple Slices](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/fries-sides/apple-slices/) | 15 kcal | Lowest-calorie side, fruit-forward |
| [Kid-sized World Famous Fries](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/fries-sides/world-famous-fries-small/) | ~110 kcal | Smaller portion than a regular small fries |

[Apple Slices](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/fries-sides/apple-slices/) at 15 kcal are the lightest choice by a wide margin. | Drink | Calories | Notes |

The [Honest Kids Juice Box](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/honest-kids-appley-ever-after-juice-box/) at 35 kcal is the lightest flavored option.

### Lowest-Calorie Happy Meal Build

The lightest practical Happy Meal choice is the **4-pc McNuggets Happy Meal** paired with **Apple Slices** and **DASANI Water**. That swap pattern trims calories compared with milk-based or fries-based builds and is the simplest way to keep the meal as light as possible while staying inside the normal Happy Meal bundle.

Because the tracked Happy Meal item pages publish bundle totals rather than every possible side-and-drink permutation, treat this as the lightest directionally correct build rather than an exact calorie promise for every location.

## McDonald's Happy Meal Menu: Frequently Asked Questions

### What is on the McDonald's Happy Meal menu in the USA?

The McDonald's Happy Meal menu currently tracks three options: the Hamburger Happy Meal ($5.89), the 4-pc McNuggets Happy Meal ($6.19), and the 6-pc McNuggets Happy Meal (~$7.29). Every Happy Meal includes a choice of Apple Slices or kid-sized fries, a kid-friendly drink, and a toy.

### How much is a McDonald's Happy Meal?

McDonald's Happy Meals range from approximately $5.89 for the Hamburger Happy Meal to $7.29 for the 6-pc McNuggets Happy Meal on the tracked USA menu. Prices vary by franchise, state, and active promotions. See the [prices by state guide](https://mcdomenuusa.com/mcdonalds-prices-by-state/) for regional context.

### How many calories are in a McDonald's Happy Meal?

The 4-pc McNuggets Happy Meal is the lightest at ~420 kcal. The Hamburger Happy Meal is ~475 kcal, and the 6-pc McNuggets Happy Meal is ~510 kcal. All figures are based on the standard build with Apple Slices and 1% Low Fat Milk. McDonald's keeps every Happy Meal at 600 calories or less.

### Can you get Apple Slices instead of fries in a Happy Meal?

Yes. Apple Slices are a standard side option in every McDonald's Happy Meal and do not require a special request. At 15 kcal they are significantly lighter than kid-sized fries (~110 kcal).

### Does every McDonald's Happy Meal come with a toy?

Yes. Every Happy Meal includes one toy. The toy theme changes with current promotions throughout the year and varies by market. It depends on the active campaign at your local restaurant.

### What is the difference between the 4-pc and 6-pc McNuggets Happy Meal?

The 4-pc McNuggets Happy Meal costs ~$6.19 and has ~420 kcal. The 6-pc version costs ~$7.29 and has ~510 kcal. The $1.10 difference buys two extra nuggets. The 4-pc is better for lighter eaters; the 6-pc suits bigger appetites.

### Which McDonald's Happy Meal is the best value?

The 4-pc McNuggets Happy Meal at ~$6.19 offers the best calorie-to-price ratio. It is the lightest meal, only $0.30 more than the Hamburger Happy Meal, and saves approximately $3.00 over ordering the same components separately.

### Are McDonald's Happy Meal prices the same everywhere?

No. Prices vary by franchise operator, state, city, local taxes, and app participation. The prices on this page are based on currently tracked USA menu data. See the [prices by state guide](https://mcdomenuusa.com/mcdonalds-prices-by-state/) for regional breakdowns.

### What drinks come with a McDonald's Happy Meal?

Happy Meal drink options include 1% Low Fat Milk Jug (100 kcal), Reduced Sugar Low Fat Chocolate Milk (130 kcal), Honest Kids Juice Box (35 kcal), and DASANI Water (0 kcal). The [beverages and drinks menu](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/) covers each drink in full detail.

### Is the McDonald's Happy Meal available at breakfast?

No. Breakfast Happy Meals are not standard in the USA. Happy Meals become available after breakfast service ends, typically around 10:30 AM on weekdays. Check [McDonald's breakfast hours](https://mcdomenuusa.com/breakfast-hours/) for timing at your location.
