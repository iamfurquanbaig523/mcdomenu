McDonald's Extra Value Meals: How They Work

Before comparing individual meals, it helps to understand exactly what you're getting — and what makes the combo math work.

What's Included in Every Extra Value Meal

Every McDonald's Extra Value Meal includes:

  • Main item (burger, sandwich, nuggets, or breakfast item)

  • Medium World Famous Fries (~320 kcal, ~$3.29 standalone)

  • Medium soft drink (~$1.69 standalone)

The meal price is almost always lower than adding those three items up individually. The effective discount is typically $1.50–$2.50 depending on the meal tier, which works out to the drink being effectively free or close to it.

Medium vs. Large Upgrade

Most locations allow a size upgrade from medium to large fries and drink for approximately $1.00 extra. That upgrade adds:

  • ~160 kcal from fries (320 kcal medium to 480 kcal large)

  • Up to ~100 kcal from the drink upgrade

  • Meaningfully more volume for $1.00

The upgrade is almost always worth it if you're already spending $9–$12 on the combo. The per-calorie and per-volume cost of the upgrade is better than any standalone add-on.

Breakfast Meals: Hash Browns Instead of Fries

Breakfast Extra Value Meals substitute Hash Browns for World Famous Fries. Hash Browns come in at 140 kcal vs. 320 kcal for medium fries — a significant calorie difference that makes breakfast combos lighter than their lunch and dinner equivalents at similar price points.

McDonald's Extra Value Meals vs. Ordering Standalone: Is the Combo Worth It?

This is the question most people are actually asking when they search for Extra Value Meal prices. The answer depends on what you're ordering and how you're ordering it.

When the Extra Value Meal Wins

The combo is worth it in almost every case where you were already planning to order fries and a drink. Here's the math on the Big Mac Meal as a benchmark:

Item Standalone Price
Big Mac ~$5.39
Medium Fries ~$3.29
Medium Soft Drink ~$1.69
Total standalone ~$10.37
Big Mac Meal price ~$9.69
Savings ~$0.68

That $0.68 saving is modest, but it compounds across the whole order. If you're feeding two people and both order meals, you're saving $1.30+ before any app deals are applied.

When Standalone or McValue Beats the Meal

  • You only want the sandwich. If you're not hungry enough for fries and a drink, the meal forces you to pay for food you won't finish. Just order the sandwich.

  • You're using McValue bundles. The McValue menu has multi-item bundles like the McChicken Meal Deal (sandwich + 4-pc nuggets + small fries + small drink) that can beat a standard Extra Value Meal on total cost if the items match what you want.

  • You have app deals active. The deals and McValue guide tracks current BOGO and discount offers. A buy-one-add-one deal on a standalone sandwich can deliver more value than a meal combo at full price.

  • You're ordering delivery. Delivery platforms mark up item prices and add fees. The McDelivery guide covers how delivery pricing affects combo value specifically.

Key takeaway: The Extra Value Meal is the right call if you want a complete order (sandwich + fries + drink) and you're not using a better deal. If you're only hungry for the sandwich, or if you have an active app promotion, recalculate before defaulting to the combo.

McDonald's Extra Value Meals: Frequently Asked Questions

What is a McDonald's Extra Value Meal?

A McDonald's Extra Value Meal is a combo that bundles a main item (burger, chicken sandwich, breakfast item, or nuggets) with medium World Famous Fries and a medium soft drink at a combined price lower than ordering those three items separately. Breakfast meals substitute Hash Browns for fries.

How much are McDonald's Extra Value Meals?

McDonald's Extra Value Meals range from approximately $6.29 for a Cheeseburger Meal up to $11.49 for a Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese Meal on the tracked USA menu. Breakfast meals run $7.59–$10.99. Chicken and fish meals run $9.49–$10.69. Prices vary by location, franchise, and state.

What is the cheapest McDonald's Extra Value Meal?

The Cheeseburger Meal at approximately $6.29 is the lowest-priced combo on the tracked USA menu. For breakfast, the Bacon, Egg & Cheese Biscuit Meal at ~$7.59 is the cheapest breakfast combo.

Can you upgrade the size on a McDonald's Extra Value Meal?

Yes. Most locations allow a medium-to-large upgrade on both fries and drink for approximately $1.00 extra. The large fries add ~160 kcal over the medium, and the drink upgrade adds volume without a significant price jump.

Are McDonald's Extra Value Meals available all day?

Lunch and dinner meals are available all day. Breakfast Extra Value Meals are only available during McDonald's breakfast hours, which typically end late morning. Availability varies by location.

How many calories are in a McDonald's Extra Value Meal?

Meal calories vary widely. Breakfast meals run ~455–825 kcal for the sandwich alone. Lunch and dinner meals with medium fries and a regular drink range from ~950 kcal (Filet-O-Fish Meal) to ~1,330 kcal (Double Quarter Pounder Meal). Swapping to Diet Coke removes up to 280 kcal from any meal total. Full data is in the nutrition and allergen guide.

Is the Big Mac Meal worth it?

At ~$9.69 for the meal vs. ~$10.37 for the three items separately, the Big Mac Meal saves approximately $0.68. It's worth ordering if you want fries and a drink with your sandwich. If you're using an app deal or only want the burger, standalone may be cheaper.

What is the difference between Extra Value Meals and the McValue menu?

McDonald's Extra Value Meals are traditional combo bundles (main + fries + drink). The McValue menu offers different bundle structures — like the McChicken Meal Deal (sandwich + 4-pc nuggets + small fries + small drink) — that may deliver more variety or lower cost depending on what you want. They serve different ordering needs.

Are McDonald's Extra Value Meal prices the same in every state?

No. Prices vary by franchise operator, state and local taxes, urban vs. rural cost structures, and app participation. The prices on this page are based on currently tracked USA menu data. See the prices by state guide for regional context.

Does McDonald's offer Extra Value Meal deals through the app?

Yes. The McDonald's app regularly features discounts on specific Extra Value Meals, including buy-one-add-one offers and percentage-off promotions. Check the deals and McValue guide for current active offers before ordering at full menu price.

Is there a fish Extra Value Meal at McDonald's?

Yes. The Filet-O-Fish Meal bundles the Filet-O-Fish sandwich with medium fries and a medium drink at approximately $9.99. It's the lowest-calorie meal in the chicken and fish tier at ~950 kcal and is especially popular during Lent.
