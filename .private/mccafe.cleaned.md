# McDonald's McCafe Coffee Menu: Prices, Calories & Drink Guide (USA 2026)

McDonald's McCafe coffee menu is one of the most underestimated value plays in the US coffee market. Premium Roast Coffee is $1.69 for any size on the current tracked USA menu. A small McCafe Iced Coffee is $2.29. Even a large Caramel Frappe - the kind of blended drink that often competes with $6-$7 specialty coffee chain drinks - is $4.89. The price gap between McCafe and premium coffee chains is real, and it compounds on every order.

This guide covers every tracked item on the McDonald's McCafe coffee menu USA, organized by drink type: basic coffee, espresso drinks, lattes, cappuccinos, macchiatos, iced coffees, frappes, and hot chocolate. ## How the McDonald's McCafe Coffee Menu Is Structured

The McDonald's McCafe coffee menu USA now splits into seven practical drink families, each with its own price tier and calorie range. Understanding that structure saves you from scanning a long list and helps you land on the right drink faster.

### The Seven Drink Families

| Drink Family | Price Range | Calorie Range | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Coffee (Premium Roast, Americano) | $1.69-$4.29 | 0-5 kcal | Lowest cost, lowest calorie |
| Hot Lattes | $3.19-$5.09 | 140-460 kcal | Creamy, espresso-forward |
| Hot Cappuccinos | $3.19-$5.19 | 110-340 kcal | Foam-forward, lighter than lattes |
| Caramel Macchiato | $3.69-$4.89 | 200-320 kcal | Layered espresso and caramel |
| Iced Coffees and Iced Lattes | $2.29-$5.09 | 140-370 kcal | Cold coffee, wide price range |
| Frappes | $3.89-$4.89 | 420-680 kcal | Blended, dessert-style |
| Hot Chocolate | $2.79-$3.89 | 270-470 kcal | Warm non-coffee option |

**The most important split:** Basic coffee (Premium Roast and Americano) sits at the value end of the menu. Hot espresso drinks - lattes, cappuccinos, and macchiatos - bring more milk, syrup, and calories into the order. Plain Iced Coffee at $2.29 gives you the lowest-entry cold option, while Frappes stretch the category into dessert-drink territory.

**Cannibalization note:** The non-coffee beverages - fountain drinks, smoothies, tea, juice, milk, and water - are covered separately on the [beverages and drinks menu](https://mcdomenuusa.com/menu/beverages-drinks/). This page covers coffee and espresso drinks only.

## Basic Coffee: Premium Roast and Americano

The two lowest-cost, lowest-calorie options on the McDonald's McCafe coffee menu are Premium Roast Coffee and the Americano. Both stay close to zero calories and land well below the price of any latte, cappuccino, or macchiato.

## McDonald's McCafe Coffee Menu: Frequently Asked Questions

### What is on the McDonald's McCafe coffee menu?

The McDonald's McCafe coffee menu USA includes Premium Roast Coffee, Americano, Lattes (classic, Caramel, French Vanilla, Mocha), Cappuccinos (classic, Caramel, French Vanilla), Caramel Macchiato, Iced Coffee, Iced Caramel Coffee, Iced French Vanilla Coffee, Iced Latte, Iced Caramel Latte, Iced French Vanilla Latte, Iced Mocha Latte, Iced Caramel Macchiato, Caramel Frappe, Mocha Frappe, and Hot Chocolate.

### How much is a McDonald's McCafe latte?

A classic McCafe Latte starts at $3.19 for a small, $4.09 for a medium, and $5.09 for a large on the current tracked USA menu. Flavored lattes in the tracked data generally run from $3.59 to $4.59 depending on flavor and size.

### How much is a McDonald's Frappe?

Both the Caramel Frappe and Mocha Frappe are priced at $3.89 small, $4.29 medium, and $4.89 large on the tracked USA menu. They are the highest-calorie drinks in the McCafe category at 420-680 kcal depending on size and flavor.

### What is the cheapest drink on the McDonald's McCafe menu?

Premium Roast Coffee at $1.69 for any size is the cheapest tracked McCafe drink. Plain Iced Coffee at $2.29 small is the cheapest cold coffee option, while the Americano starts at $2.39 for readers who want an espresso-based drink.

### What is the difference between a McCafe latte and cappuccino?

A latte uses more steamed milk and less foam, making it creamier and usually higher in calories. A cappuccino uses more foam and less milk, making it lighter and airier at similar sizes. A classic Cappuccino large is 200 kcal vs. a classic Latte large at 240 kcal in the current tracked data.

### Does McCafe iced coffee come sweetened?

Yes. The plain Iced Coffee comes sweetened by default. The Iced Latte and other espresso-led cold drinks give you a cleaner base if you want more control over sweetness.

### What is the lowest-calorie McCafe drink?

Premium Roast Coffee and the Americano are the lowest-calorie options on the McDonald's McCafe coffee menu, staying in the 0-5 kcal range on the current tracked site data. Among the heavier iced options, the lighter choice is usually to stay with plain Iced Coffee or move away from syrup-based builds.

### Are McDonald's McCafe prices the same everywhere?

No. Prices vary by franchise operator, state, city, local taxes, and app participation. The prices on this page are based on currently tracked USA menu data. See the [prices by state guide](https://mcdomenuusa.com/mcdonalds-prices-by-state/) for regional context.
