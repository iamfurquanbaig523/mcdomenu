McDonald's Fruit and Maple Oatmeal costs $3.79 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $2.99 in Mississippi to $4.99 in Hawaii. It has 320 calories — one of the healthiest breakfast items on the menu, made with whole-grain Quaker oats and real fruit.

Fruit & Maple Oatmeal Price by State (2026)

What's in the Oatmeal?

Whole-grain Quaker oats cooked with light cream, topped with diced red and green apples, a cranberry-raisin blend, and two pumps of brown sugar maple-flavored syrup. Made fresh to order. You can customize it — ask for no cream (saves 30 cal), no sugar (saves 30 cal), or no fruit. Without cream and sugar, it drops to about 260 calories.

Nutrition Facts

Oatmeal vs Other Breakfast Options

The oatmeal has the lowest fat of any breakfast item (4.5g) and the highest fiber (5g). The Egg McMuffin has similar calories but triple the fat. Hotcakes have nearly double the calories. If you want the healthiest breakfast at McDonald's, the oatmeal wins.

Availability and Deals

Available during breakfast hours only — typically until 10:30 AM weekdays, 11:00 AM weekends. Pair with a black coffee for a breakfast under 330 calories. The McDonald's app runs breakfast promotions regularly.
