A 20-Piece Chicken McNuggets costs $8.99 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $7.99 in Mississippi to $11.49 in Hawaii. It has 830 calories and 44g of protein — the best value McNuggets size and the go-to choice for sharing or feeding a group.

20-Piece McNuggets Price by State (2026)

What You Get

Twenty white-meat chicken nuggets made from chicken breast, rib, and tenderloin, seasoned and coated in a light tempura batter. You get three dipping sauces included. Each nugget comes in one of four shapes — bell, boot, ball, and bone — designed for even cooking. Popular sauce choices include Tangy BBQ, Sweet 'N Sour, Honey Mustard, and the spicy Buffalo sauce. See all options on the sauces page.

Nutrition Facts

The 20-piece has 44g of protein — nearly as much as a Double Quarter Pounder (48g). If two people split it, each gets 415 calories and 22g protein, making it a reasonable shared meal. Each dipping sauce adds 30-110 calories — Tangy BBQ adds 45 cal, Ranch adds 110 cal.

McNuggets Size Comparison

The 20-piece at $0.45 per nugget saves you $0.10 per nugget compared to the 10-piece. Ordering one 20-piece instead of two 10-piece boxes saves about $1.99. The 40-Piece at $0.32 per nugget is even better value for larger groups — you save 42% per nugget versus buying individual 10-piece boxes.

When to Order the 20-Piece

The 20-piece works best for sharing between two to three people, for parties, or for meal prepping. At 830 calories, eating the whole box solo is a full meal and then some. If ordering for two, one 20-piece ($8.99) plus Large Fries ($3.19) to share comes to about $12.18 — roughly $6 per person.

Deals and Tips

The McDonald's app regularly offers free 6-piece McNuggets with a minimum purchase — one of the most common promotions. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward free McNuggets. McNuggets reheat well in an air fryer at 375°F for 3-4 minutes, making the 20-piece a solid meal prep option. Check the McValue menu for bundle deals.
