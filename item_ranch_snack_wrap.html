What's in a Ranch Snack Wrap?

A crispy white-meat chicken strip wrapped in a soft flour tortilla with shredded lettuce, a slice of American cheese, and creamy ranch sauce. The chicken strip is the same crispy strip used in McCrispy Strips. The ranch sauce is the same Creamy Ranch available as a dipping sauce — smooth and tangy.

Nutrition Facts

Ranch vs Spicy Snack Wrap

Same chicken, same tortilla, same price. Ranch has 10 more calories from the creamier dressing. Choose ranch if you prefer mild and creamy, spicy if you want tangy heat. Kids and people who avoid spicy food tend to prefer the ranch version.

Best Combos and Deals

Two Ranch Snack Wraps ($5.98) make a filling 700-calorie meal with 30g protein. One wrap plus Small Fries ($1.59) is a light combo under $5. The Ranch Snack Wrap is easy to eat one-handed, making it ideal for drive-through. Check the McDonald's app for wrap deals.
