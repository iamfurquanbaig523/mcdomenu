McDonald's Apple Slices cost $0.99 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $0.49 in Mississippi to $1.49 in Hawaii. They have just 15 calories — the lowest-calorie item on the entire McDonald's menu and the default side in every Happy Meal.

Apple Slices Price by State (2026)

What Are McDonald's Apple Slices?

Pre-sliced, skin-on Gala apple pieces in a small bag. Treated with calcium ascorbate (calcium + vitamin C) to prevent browning. No added sugar, no caramel dip, no seasoning — just plain apple slices. McDonald's removed the caramel dip option years ago to make the item healthier for kids.

Nutrition Facts

Apple Slices vs Other Sides

Apple Slices have 15x fewer calories than Small Fries. Swapping fries for Apple Slices in any meal saves 200+ calories. They are the only side with zero fat and zero sodium.

Who Orders Apple Slices?

Mostly parents ordering Happy Meals — Apple Slices come included as the default side alongside small fries. Adults watching calories also order them as a stand-alone snack under $1. They are available all day, unlike Hash Browns which are breakfast-only.
