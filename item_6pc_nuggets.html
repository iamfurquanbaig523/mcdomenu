A 6-Piece Chicken McNuggets costs $3.49 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $2.79 in Mississippi to $4.69 in Hawaii. It has 250 calories — the mid-size nugget order that hits the sweet spot between a snack and a light meal.

6-Piece McNuggets Price by State (2026)

What You Get

Six white-meat chicken nuggets made from breast, rib, and tenderloin, seasoned, battered, and fried until golden. You get one dipping sauce — popular choices include Tangy BBQ, Sweet 'N Sour, Honey Mustard, and Spicy Buffalo. See all options on the sauces page.

Nutrition Facts

McNuggets Size Comparison

The 6-Piece at $0.58 per nugget is better value than the 4-Piece ($0.62). For $2 more, the 10-Piece gives you 4 extra nuggets and a second sauce — the best step-up if you want a full meal.

Best Combos and Deals

A 6-Piece with Small Fries ($1.59) makes a 480-calorie combo for about $5.08 — a solid light lunch. The 6-Piece McNuggets Meal pairs them with medium fries and a drink. It is also the nugget option in the Happy Meal. The McDonald's app frequently offers a free 6-piece with any purchase — one of the most common app deals.
