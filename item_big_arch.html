It has approximately 590 calories and 38g of protein — McDonald's newest premium burger with two quarter-pound fresh beef patties, launched to compete with the Whopper and Dave's Single.

Big Arch Price by State (2026)

What's in a Big Arch?

Two 1/4 lb fresh-beef patties cooked to order on a flat iron grill, three slices of American cheese, a tangy special sauce, crispy toppings for crunch, shredded lettuce, pickles, and diced onions on a toasted sesame seed bun. The Big Arch uses quarter-pound patties — much larger than the Big Mac's smaller 1/10 lb patties — and has no middle bun.

Nutrition Facts

Big Arch vs Big Mac vs Double QPC

The Big Arch and Big Mac have the same calories (590) but the Big Arch has 13g more protein because of the larger patties. The Big Arch and Double QPC are the same price ($7.49) with the same amount of beef, but the Big Arch has 150 fewer calories thanks to the tangy sauce and crunch toppings replacing the DQPC's extra cheese. The Big Arch sits right between the Big Mac and Double QPC — more beef than the Mac, fewer calories than the DQPC.

Meal and Deals

The Big Arch Meal with medium fries and drink costs about $11.49-$12.49. Available all day at participating US locations. The McDonald's app runs Big Arch launch promotions and bundle deals. Check the McValue menu for combo options.
