A McDonald's Double Cheeseburger costs $3.49 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $2.79 in Mississippi to $4.69 in Hawaii. It has 450 calories and 25g of protein — the cheesiest value burger on the menu with two patties and two slices of American cheese.

Double Cheeseburger Price by State (2026)

What's in a Double Cheeseburger?

Two 100% beef patties (1.6 oz each before cooking) with two slices of melted American cheese, pickles, diced onions, ketchup, and mustard on a regular toasted bun. The extra cheese slice between the patties is the only difference between this and a McDouble, making it noticeably cheesier.

Nutrition Facts

Double Cheeseburger vs McDouble

The $0.60 difference buys you an extra slice of American cheese — 50 more calories and 3g more protein. If cheese is not a priority, the McDouble is the better deal. If you want maximum cheese-to-meat ratio in a value burger, the Double Cheeseburger delivers. Both use the same bun, same patties, same toppings.

Deals and Tips

The Double Cheeseburger appears in McValue promotions and Buy 1 Add 1 for $1 deals. Two Double Cheeseburgers plus Small Fries makes a filling meal for about $8.50. The McDonald's app runs exclusive bundle pricing on double burgers regularly.
