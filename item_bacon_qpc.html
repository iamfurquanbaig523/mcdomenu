What's in a Bacon Quarter Pounder?

A 1/4 lb fresh beef patty cooked to order on a flat iron grill, two slices of American cheese, thick-cut Applewood smoked bacon strips, slivered onions, dill pickles, ketchup, and mustard on a toasted sesame seed bun. Like all Quarter Pounders, the beef is fresh — never frozen. The bacon is thick-cut, not the thin strips used at most fast food chains.

Bacon QPC vs Other Quarter Pounders

The Bacon QPC and Double QPC are the same price at $7.49. The Double QPC gives you double the beef (1/2 lb) with 120 more calories. The Bacon QPC gives you smoky bacon flavor but half the meat. If you are hungry, go Double QPC. If you want flavor variety and that smoky bacon crunch, go Bacon QPC.

Meal and Deals

The Bacon QPC Meal with medium fries and drink costs about $10.99-$11.99. The McDonald's app frequently runs Quarter Pounder line deals. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward a free QPC at 4000 points. Check the McValue menu for bundle pricing.
