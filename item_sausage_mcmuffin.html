What's in a Sausage McMuffin?

A seasoned pork sausage patty with a slice of American cheese on a toasted English muffin. No egg, no vegetables, no sauce — just sausage and cheese. The sausage is seasoned with salt, pepper, and sage. The English muffin is the same nooks-and-crannies style used across all McMuffin sandwiches, toasted until the edges are crisp.

Nutrition Facts

Sausage McMuffin vs Other McMuffins

The Sausage McMuffin costs half the price of the Egg McMuffin but has 90 more calories because the sausage patty is fattier than Canadian bacon. Interestingly, the Sausage Burrito at the same $2.29 has fewer calories (310) and includes egg — making it the better budget pick if you want egg in your breakfast.

Deals and Availability

The Sausage McMuffin is a staple on the McValue menu and appears in Buy 1 Add 1 for $1 breakfast deals. A Sausage McMuffin ($2.29) plus Hash Browns ($1.99) makes a $4.28 breakfast. Available during breakfast hours only. Check the McDonald's app for promotions.
