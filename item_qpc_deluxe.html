What's in a QPC Deluxe?

A 1/4 lb fresh beef patty cooked to order on a flat iron grill, two slices of American cheese, crisp leaf lettuce, Roma tomato slices, slivered onions, dill pickles, mayo, ketchup, and mustard on a toasted sesame seed bun. The Deluxe adds three things the regular Quarter Pounder does not have: lettuce, tomato, and mayonnaise.

QPC Deluxe vs Other Quarter Pounders

The QPC Deluxe adds $0.90 over the regular Quarter Pounder for fresh toppings — a reasonable upgrade for a more complete burger. The Bacon QPC at $7.49 trades vegetables for smoky bacon. Choose the Deluxe if you want freshness and crunch, the Bacon if you want richness and salt.

Meal and Deals

The QPC Deluxe Meal with medium fries and drink costs about $10.49-$11.49. The McDonald's app frequently runs Quarter Pounder deals. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward a free QPC. Check the McValue menu for bundle options.
