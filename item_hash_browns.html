McDonald's Hash Browns cost $1.99 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.49 in Mississippi to $2.99 in Hawaii. They have just 140 calories and are the most popular breakfast side at McDonald's — crispy on the outside, fluffy on the inside.

What's in McDonald's Hash Browns?

Shredded potatoes formed into an oval patty, seasoned with salt, dehydrated potato, and corn flour for binding, then fried until golden and crispy. The outside gets a crunchy shell while the inside stays soft and fluffy. McDonald's Hash Browns are cooked in vegetable oil but contain natural beef flavor derived from hydrolyzed wheat and hydrolyzed milk — meaning they are not vegan or dairy-free despite being a potato product.

Nutrition Facts

Hash Browns are low in calories but also low in protein at just 1g. They work best paired with a protein-rich sandwich. Allergens include wheat and milk (from the natural beef flavor). See our allergen guide.

Hash Browns vs Other Breakfast Sides

The Hash Brown is the best value breakfast side — 140 calories of crispy potato for $1.99. Hotcakes cost over double and have four times the calories. Apple Slices are cheaper but do not satisfy the same craving for something hot and crunchy.

Best Breakfast Combos with Hash Browns

Hash Browns are included in the $4 Breakfast Meal Deal from the McValue menu — a breakfast sandwich, hash brown, and small coffee for $4 flat. That is the best breakfast deal at any fast food chain. Hash Browns are also included in every breakfast combo meal on the Extra Value Meals menu. If ordering separately, a Sausage McMuffin ($2.29) plus Hash Brown ($1.99) comes to $4.28 — a filling breakfast under $5.

Availability

Hash Browns are only available during breakfast hours — typically until 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends. McDonald's ended all-day breakfast in 2020. If you want a potato side during lunch, Small Fries ($1.59) are your closest option. Check the McDonald's app for breakfast promotions.
