A McDonald's Sausage Burrito costs $2.29 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.79 in Mississippi to $3.29 in Hawaii. It has 310 calories — one of the cheapest breakfast items with both meat and egg, and the only breakfast item with green chiles for a touch of heat.

Sausage Burrito Price by State (2026)

What's in a Sausage Burrito?

Scrambled eggs, pork sausage crumbles, melted American cheese, green chiles, and diced onions wrapped in a soft flour tortilla. The green chiles give it a mild kick that sets it apart from every other McDonald's breakfast sandwich. It is one of the few breakfast options with any kind of spice or Southwest flavor on the breakfast menu.

Nutrition Facts

Sausage Burrito vs Other Budget Breakfast Items

The Sausage Burrito and Sausage McMuffin are the same price ($2.29), but the burrito has 90 fewer calories and includes egg while the McMuffin does not. The burrito is the better choice if you want fewer calories with more variety of ingredients. The McMuffin has slightly more protein (14g vs 13g) and a heartier, denser feel.

Best Budget Breakfast Strategy

Two Sausage Burritos for $4.58 give you 620 calories and 26g of protein — a complete breakfast under $5. The Sausage Burrito Meal on the Extra Value Meals menu gives you two burritos, hash browns, and a small drink. The $4 Breakfast Meal Deal from the McValue menu is another strong option — a breakfast sandwich, hash brown, and small coffee for $4 flat.

Availability and Deals

Available during breakfast hours only — typically until 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends. The McDonald's app runs Buy One Add One for $1 breakfast promotions regularly. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward free breakfast items.
