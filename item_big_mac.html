What's in a Big Mac?

Two 1/10 lb beef patties, special Big Mac sauce, shredded lettuce, American cheese, pickles, and diced onions on a three-part sesame seed bun (crown, club, and heel). The Big Mac sauce is a tangy, creamy blend similar to Thousand Island dressing with relish, mustard, and vinegar. The three-piece bun with a middle "club" layer is unique to the Big Mac and creates that signature layered eating experience.

Nutrition Facts

The Big Mac contains wheat, milk, and soy allergens. The sauce contains egg. It is not suitable for gluten-free, dairy-free, or egg-free diets. For allergen details on all items, see our allergen guide.

Big Mac vs Other Burgers

The Big Mac is really about the sauce and the bun, not the beef. It actually has less meat than a Quarter Pounder — two small patties versus one larger one. If you want more beef for less money, a McDouble at $2.79 has the same amount of meat as a Big Mac at half the price. The Big Mac's premium is the sauce, the three-piece bun, and the lettuce.

Big Mac Meal Price

The Big Mac Meal includes a Big Mac, medium fries, and a medium drink for about $9.99-$10.99 depending on location. Upgrading to large fries and a large drink adds about $1.00-$1.50. Ordering the items separately costs about $11.50-$12.50, so the meal deal saves you $1.50-$2.00 on average.

How to Save Money on a Big Mac

The McDonald's app regularly offers Big Mac deals — free Big Mac with $1 minimum purchase for first-time app users, BOGO deals, and points rewards through MyMcDonald's Rewards (1500 points = free Big Mac). The McValue menu occasionally includes Big Mac bundles. You can also order a McDouble and ask for Mac sauce and lettuce — some call this a "Poor Man's Big Mac" and it costs about $3.50 with the add-ons versus $5.99 for the real thing.

Big Mac History and Fun Facts

The Big Mac was invented by Jim Delligatti, a McDonald's franchisee in Uniontown, Pennsylvania, in 1967. It went nationwide in 1968. The original price was $0.45. Adjusted for inflation, that $0.45 in 1968 equals about $4.10 in 2026 — meaning today's Big Mac at $5.99 costs about 46% more than it did at launch in real dollars. The Big Mac Index, published by The Economist since 1986, uses Big Mac prices worldwide to compare purchasing power between countries.
