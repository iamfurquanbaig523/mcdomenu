A 4-Piece Chicken McNuggets costs $2.49 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.99 in Mississippi to $3.49 in Hawaii. It has just 170 calories — the smallest McNuggets order, perfect as a snack, side, or part of a value meal deal.

4-Piece McNuggets Price by State (2026)

What You Get

Four white-meat chicken nuggets made from breast, rib, and tenderloin, seasoned, battered, and fried. You get one dipping sauce. The nuggets come in four shapes — bell, ball, boot, and bone — designed for even cooking and consistent crunch.

Nutrition Facts

McNuggets Size Comparison

The 4-Piece has the highest per-nugget cost at $0.62. If you are hungry for more than 4, upgrading to the 6-Piece ($3.49) or 10-Piece ($5.49) saves money per nugget. The 4-Piece is best as a snack or side dish rather than a main course.

Deals and Combos

The 4-Piece is included in the $5 Meal Deal alongside a sandwich, small fries, and small drink — making it part of one of the best value meals at any fast food chain. It is also a Happy Meal option. The McDonald's app often offers free 6-piece McNuggets with any purchase — better value than buying a 4-piece.
