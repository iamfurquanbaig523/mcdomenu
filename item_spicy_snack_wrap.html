What's in a Spicy Snack Wrap?

A crispy chicken strip (same as McCrispy Strips) wrapped in a soft flour tortilla with shredded lettuce, a slice of American cheese, and spicy buffalo sauce. The chicken strip gives a satisfying crunch while the buffalo sauce adds a tangy heat. It is essentially a mini chicken sandwich in wrap form.

Nutrition Facts

Spicy vs Ranch Snack Wrap

Same price, same chicken strip, same tortilla — the only difference is the sauce. The Ranch version has 10 more calories from the creamier ranch dressing. Choose spicy if you like heat, ranch if you prefer mild and creamy.

Snack Wrap vs Full Chicken Sandwiches

The Snack Wrap has fewer calories than a McChicken and costs $0.70 more. Two wraps ($5.98) give you 680 calories and 30g protein — comparable to a McCrispy meal but with more variety. Check the McDonald's app for wrap deals.
