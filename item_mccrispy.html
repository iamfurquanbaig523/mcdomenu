A McCrispy costs $5.49 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $4.79 in Mississippi to $6.99 in Hawaii. It has 470 calories and is McDonald's premium chicken sandwich — their direct answer to Chick-fil-A and Popeyes in the chicken sandwich wars.

What's in a McCrispy?

A thick, whole white-meat chicken breast fillet, seasoned and pressure-cooked in a crunchy breading, served with crinkle-cut pickles on a toasted potato roll. The potato roll is softer and slightly sweet compared to the regular buns used on burgers. The chicken breast is a single whole piece — not a processed patty like the McChicken. McDonald's launched the McCrispy in 2021 to compete directly with Chick-fil-A's original sandwich and Popeyes' chicken sandwich.

Nutrition Facts

McCrispy vs Competitors

The McCrispy is the lowest-calorie option among the major chain chicken sandwiches. Popeyes' version has 230 more calories. However, the McCrispy also has the least protein at 21g. Chick-fil-A's sandwich has 28g protein in fewer calories — nutritionally the best option. The McCrispy wins on availability since McDonald's has roughly 13,500 US locations versus Chick-fil-A's 3,000.

McCrispy Variations

McDonald's offers four McCrispy sandwiches. The regular McCrispy ($5.49) has chicken and pickles. The Spicy McCrispy ($5.49) adds cayenne pepper heat to the breading. The Deluxe McCrispy ($6.29) adds lettuce, tomato, and mayo. The Spicy Deluxe ($6.29) combines spice with fresh toppings. If you want the crispiest, cleanest chicken taste, the regular McCrispy with just pickles is the way to go.

McCrispy Meal Price and Deals

The McCrispy Meal with medium fries and a medium drink costs about $9.49-$10.49. The McDonald's app frequently runs McCrispy BOGO deals and free McCrispy with minimum purchase offers. If budget is the priority, the McChicken at $2.29 gives you a chicken sandwich at less than half the price — just with a smaller, processed patty instead of a whole breast.
