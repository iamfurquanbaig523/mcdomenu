A McDouble costs $2.79 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $2.29 in Mississippi to $3.99 in Hawaii. It has 400 calories and is widely considered the best value burger on the McDonald's menu — two beef patties and cheese for under $3.

What's in a McDouble?

Two 100% beef patties (1/10 lb each before cooking), one slice of American cheese, dill pickles, diced onions, ketchup, and mustard on a regular toasted bun. The McDouble is essentially a Double Cheeseburger with one less slice of cheese. That one missing cheese slice is the only difference — same patties, same bun, same toppings — but saves you $0.50.

McDouble vs Similar Burgers

At $1.40 per patty, the McDouble is the cheapest way to get two beef patties at McDonald's. The Big Mac has the same amount of meat (two 1/10 lb patties) but costs $5.99 — more than double the price. The extra $3.20 buys you Big Mac sauce, lettuce, and the three-piece bun. Many budget-conscious customers order a McDouble with Mac sauce added (about $0.30-$0.50 extra) as a cheaper alternative to the Big Mac.

McDouble Meal Deal

The McDouble Meal Deal bundles a McDouble, 4-piece McNuggets, small fries, and a small drink for about $5.99. This is one of the best-value combos on the menu — you get a burger, nuggets, fries, and a drink for the price of a single Big Mac. The McDouble also appears in the Buy 1 Add 1 for $1 lunch promotion, letting you get two McDoubles for about $3.79.

How to Customize

Popular McDouble customizations include adding Mac sauce ($0.30), adding lettuce ($0.20), adding bacon ($1.50), or subbing the bun for a lettuce wrap (free, for low-carb). You can also order it "like a Big Mac" — add lettuce, Mac sauce, and sub a sesame bun — for about $3.50 total versus $5.99 for the real thing.
