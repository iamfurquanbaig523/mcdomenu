A McDonald's Hot Caramel Sundae costs $2.89 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $2.29 in Mississippi to $3.99 in Hawaii. It has 340 calories — warm buttery caramel sauce over cold vanilla soft-serve, the sweeter alternative to the Hot Fudge Sundae.

What's in a Hot Caramel Sundae?

McDonald's vanilla soft-serve ice cream in a plastic cup, topped with warm buttery caramel sauce. The caramel has a sweet, slightly salty, toffee-like flavor. The temperature contrast between warm sauce and cold ice cream is what makes it work. No extra toppings — just ice cream and caramel.

Hot Caramel vs Hot Fudge vs Other Desserts

The Hot Caramel and Hot Fudge sundaes are the same price with nearly identical calories. Choose caramel if you prefer butterscotch/toffee flavors, fudge if you prefer chocolate. Some locations will do a swirl of both sauces for no extra charge — just ask.

Ice Cream Machine and Tips

Like all ice cream items, the sundae depends on the soft-serve machine. Check the McDonald's app before making a trip — it shows availability. Pair with a Baked Apple Pie ($1.89) for an apple-caramel combo under $5. The McDonald's app occasionally runs dessert promotions.
