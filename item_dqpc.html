A Double Quarter Pounder with Cheese costs $7.49 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $6.69 in Mississippi to $9.49 in Hawaii. It has 740 calories and 48g of protein — the highest-protein item on the entire McDonald's menu. If you want the most beef in a single sandwich, this is it.

What's in a Double Quarter Pounder?

Two quarter-pound (4 oz each, half-pound total) fresh-beef patties cooked to order on a flat iron grill, two slices of American cheese, slivered onions, pickles, ketchup, and mustard on a sesame seed bun. Like all Quarter Pounders, the beef is fresh — never frozen. You get a full half-pound of beef before cooking, which shrinks to roughly 6.4 ounces after grilling.

Nutrition Facts

At 48g of protein, this single burger covers nearly your entire daily protein need. However, it also maxes out your daily saturated fat at 100% DV. Allergens include wheat, milk, and soy. See our allergen guide for details.

Double Quarter Pounder vs Other Heavy Burgers

The Double QPC and the Bacon QPC are the same price at $7.49, which creates an interesting choice. The Double QPC gives you double the beef (half-pound vs quarter-pound) with 120 more calories. The Bacon QPC gives you bacon and a smokier flavor but half the meat. If you are hungry, go Double QPC. If you want flavor variety, go Bacon QPC. Compared to Wendy's Baconator ($8.49), the Double QPC is $1 cheaper with 210 fewer calories.

Double Quarter Pounder Meal Price

The DQPC Meal with medium fries and a medium drink costs about $11.49-$12.49 — one of the priciest meals on the Extra Value Meals menu. The meal saves about $1.50-$2.00 versus ordering separately. Some locations offer a large meal upgrade for $1.00-$1.50 more. If budget is a concern, two McDoubles at $5.58 total give you four patties (smaller ones) for less than one Double QPC.

How to Save Money

The Double QPC appears in McDonald's app BOGO deals and MyMcDonald's Rewards (3000 points = free DQPC). The McValue menu occasionally runs Quarter Pounder family bundles. Ordering through the app versus at the counter often unlocks $1-$2 off meal pricing.
