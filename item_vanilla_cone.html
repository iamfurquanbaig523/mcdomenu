A McDonald's Vanilla Cone costs $1.89 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.39 in Mississippi to $2.79 in Hawaii. It has just 200 calories — the cheapest and lowest-calorie dessert on the entire McDonald's menu.

What's in a Vanilla Cone?

McDonald's vanilla soft-serve ice cream swirled into a crispy cake cone. The soft-serve is made from reduced-fat vanilla ice cream with a smooth, creamy texture. It is dispensed from the same machine used for McFlurries and sundaes. The cone is a standard wafer-style cake cone, not a waffle cone. McDonald's soft-serve has been a menu staple since the 1970s and uses the same recipe across all US locations.

Nutrition Facts

Vanilla Cone vs Other Desserts

The Vanilla Cone costs less than half the price of a McFlurry and has less than half the calories. It is the most calorie-efficient dessert on the menu. If you want chocolate, the Hot Fudge Sundae at $2.89 adds hot fudge over the same soft-serve for 130 more calories. The Baked Apple Pie at the same $1.89 price point gives you a warm alternative with less sugar.

Ice Cream Machine Availability

The McDonald's ice cream machine is famously unreliable. Check the McDonald's app before making a trip specifically for a Vanilla Cone — the app shows whether ice cream items are available at your nearest location. The machine runs an automatic 4-hour cleaning cycle, usually late at night, so your best chance is during regular daytime hours between 11 AM and 9 PM.

Tips and Deals

The Vanilla Cone pairs well with a Baked Apple Pie ($1.89) for a classic dessert combo under $4. The McDonald's app occasionally offers free cone promotions. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward free desserts. At under $2, the Vanilla Cone is already cheap enough that deals are a bonus rather than a necessity.
