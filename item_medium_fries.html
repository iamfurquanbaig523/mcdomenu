Nutrition Facts

Fries Size Comparison

Medium gives you 56% more fries than Small for $1.20 more. The Large is actually the best value per ounce at $0.54, but it packs 490 calories. Medium is the best balance of portion size and calories for most people eating a full meal.

Deals and Tips

Medium Fries are the default in all Extra Value Meals. The McDonald's app frequently runs free fries promotions, often on Fridays. Ask for fries with no salt if you want them fresh — then add your own. Fries are best eaten within 5-7 minutes of serving.
