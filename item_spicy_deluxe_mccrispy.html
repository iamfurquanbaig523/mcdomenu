What's in a Spicy Deluxe McCrispy?

A whole white-meat chicken breast fillet coated in spicy cayenne and chili pepper breading, pressure-cooked until crispy, topped with shredded lettuce, Roma tomato slices, and mayonnaise on a toasted potato roll. It combines the spice of the Spicy McCrispy with the fresh toppings of the Deluxe McCrispy.

Nutrition Facts

How Spicy Is It?

About a 4-5 out of 10. The cayenne and chili pepper blend creates a slow-building warmth, not an immediate burn. It is spicier than Wendy's Spicy Chicken but milder than Popeyes' Spicy Chicken. The lettuce, tomato, and mayo help cool the heat. If you find it too spicy, order ranch sauce on the side to cut it further.

All Four McCrispy Sandwiches

The Spicy Deluxe is the "everything" option — heat plus fresh toppings. At the same $6.29 as the non-spicy Deluxe, the spice is a free upgrade. If you like any level of heat, this is the best McCrispy to order.

Meal and Deals

The Spicy Deluxe McCrispy Meal with medium fries and drink costs about $10.29-$11.29. The McDonald's app runs McCrispy BOGO deals. For a budget alternative, the McChicken at $2.29 is a third of the price.
