What's in a Sausage Biscuit?

A seasoned pork sausage patty inside a warm, buttermilk biscuit baked in the restaurant. No cheese, no egg — just sausage and biscuit. The biscuit has a buttery, flaky texture with a slightly crispy outside. The sausage is the same patty used in the Sausage McMuffin, flavored with sage, pepper, and salt.

Nutrition Facts

Sausage Biscuit vs Sausage McMuffin

Same price, different experience. The biscuit is richer and more buttery but has 60 more calories, less protein, and no cheese. The McMuffin is lighter with cheese included. Choose the biscuit if you prefer a Southern-style, flaky bread. Choose the McMuffin if you want cheese and fewer calories.

Deals and Availability

The Sausage Biscuit appears in McValue deals and Buy 1 Add 1 for $1 breakfast promotions. Available during breakfast hours only. Biscuit availability varies by region — some northern US locations may not carry biscuit items. Check the McDonald's app to confirm.
