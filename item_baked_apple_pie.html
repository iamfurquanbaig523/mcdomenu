A McDonald's Baked Apple Pie costs $1.89 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.39 in Mississippi to $2.79 in Hawaii. It has just 230 calories — one of the cheapest and lowest-calorie desserts on the menu, and a McDonald's classic since the 1960s.

What's in a McDonald's Apple Pie?

Diced American-grown apples in a cinnamon-sugar sauce wrapped in a flaky, lattice-style pastry crust and baked until golden. McDonald's switched from a deep-fried apple pie to this baked version in 1992. The baked version has a crispier, lighter crust with a caramelized sugar coating on top. It is served warm and can be extremely hot inside when fresh — let it cool for a minute before biting in.

Nutrition Facts

The Apple Pie has the lowest sugar content of any dessert on the menu at just 13g — compared to 24g in a Vanilla Cone, 43g in a Hot Fudge Sundae, and 64g in an Oreo McFlurry. Allergens include wheat, milk, and soy.

Apple Pie vs Other Desserts

The Apple Pie and Vanilla Cone tie as the cheapest desserts at $1.89 each. The Apple Pie is the only warm dessert option and has less sugar than any ice cream-based item. If you want something sweet but not ice cream, the Apple Pie is your only choice at McDonald's.

Best Dessert Combos

The classic pairing is an Apple Pie with a Vanilla Cone — warm cinnamon filling with cold soft-serve for $3.78 total. Some customers dip the pie into the soft-serve or crumble it into a Hot Fudge Sundae. Pair it with a hot coffee for an afternoon snack under $4.

Tips and Deals

The Apple Pie does not depend on the ice cream machine, so it is always available when the restaurant is open. At $1.89 it is already one of the cheapest items on the menu. The McDonald's app occasionally offers 2-for-1 apple pie deals. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward free desserts.
