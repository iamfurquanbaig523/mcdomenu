A Quarter Pounder with Cheese costs $5.79 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $5.09 in Mississippi to $7.49 in Hawaii. It has 520 calories and is the only McDonald's burger made with fresh, never-frozen beef at most locations.

What's in a Quarter Pounder with Cheese?

A single 1/4 lb (4 oz before cooking) fresh beef patty cooked on a flat iron grill, two slices of melted American cheese, slivered onions, dill pickles, ketchup, and mustard on a toasted sesame seed bun. The beef is fresh — never frozen — and cooked to order when you place your order. This makes it taste noticeably different from the frozen-patty burgers like the Big Mac and McDouble. The two slices of cheese (versus one on most burgers) add extra creaminess.

Quarter Pounder vs Other Burgers

The Quarter Pounder is $0.20 cheaper than a Big Mac while having more protein (30g vs 25g) and fewer calories (520 vs 590). The key difference is the QPC uses fresh beef and a simpler build, while the Big Mac relies on its special sauce and three-piece bun. If you care about beef quality and quantity, the QPC is the better burger. If you want the iconic sauce experience, go Big Mac.

Quarter Pounder Meal Price

A QPC Meal with medium fries and a medium drink costs about $9.79-$10.79 depending on location. That is about $0.50-$1.00 less than a Big Mac Meal at most locations. Upgrading to large costs about $1.00-$1.50 extra. The meal saves you roughly $1.50-$2.00 compared to ordering each item separately.

Quarter Pounder Variations

McDonald's offers four Quarter Pounder variations. The regular QPC ($5.79) is the base model. The QPC Deluxe ($6.69) adds lettuce, tomato, and mayo — a $0.90 upgrade for a more complete burger. The Bacon QPC ($7.49) adds thick-cut Applewood smoked bacon — the richest option. The Double QPC ($7.49) doubles the beef to a half-pound — the most filling option at 740 calories and 48g protein.

How to Save Money

The Quarter Pounder line appears frequently in McDonald's app deals. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward a free QPC (1500 points). The McValue menu sometimes includes QPC meal bundles at a discount. Ordering through the app versus in-store often unlocks exclusive pricing.
