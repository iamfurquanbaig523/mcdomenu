A Filet-O-Fish costs $5.29 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $4.49 in Mississippi to $6.99 in Hawaii. It has 390 calories and is the only fish sandwich on the McDonald's menu — a favorite during Lent and for customers who avoid beef and chicken.

What's in a Filet-O-Fish?

A wild-caught Alaska Pollock fillet coated in crispy breading, topped with a half slice of American cheese and tartar sauce on a steamed bun. The Filet-O-Fish has been on the menu since 1962 — invented by franchise owner Lou Groen in Cincinnati to serve Catholic customers during Lent who could not eat meat on Fridays. The steamed bun is unique to this sandwich and gives it a softer, pillowy texture compared to the toasted buns on burgers. The tartar sauce is a creamy blend of mayo, relish, and onion.

Nutrition Facts

The Filet-O-Fish has the lowest sodium of any sandwich at McDonald's — just 580mg compared to 1050mg in a Big Mac. The tartar sauce accounts for about 70 calories. Ordering without tartar sauce drops the sandwich to roughly 320 calories. Allergens include fish, wheat, milk, egg, and soy. See our allergen guide.

Filet-O-Fish vs Other Sandwiches

The Filet-O-Fish has fewer calories and less fat than most McDonald's sandwiches. However, at $5.29 for just 16g of protein, it is not the best value. A McDouble gives you more protein (22g) for $2.89. The Filet-O-Fish makes sense if you want fish specifically or need to avoid beef and chicken — otherwise, other sandwiches offer more food for less money.

Filet-O-Fish Meal and Deals

The Filet-O-Fish Meal with medium fries and a medium drink costs about $8.99-$9.49. During Lent (February through April), McDonald's runs Filet-O-Fish promotions through the McDonald's app including BOGO deals and discounted meals. Some locations offer a Double Filet-O-Fish for about $6.99-$7.49 — two fish patties in one bun.

Tips for Ordering

Ask for extra tartar sauce (free) if you like it creamy, or order without tartar sauce to save 70 calories. Request it cooked fresh — the Filet-O-Fish sits in the warming cabinet longer than burgers because it sells slower, so a fresh one makes a real difference. The McDonald's app often has free Filet-O-Fish offers during Lent and on Fridays year-round. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward a free Filet-O-Fish.
