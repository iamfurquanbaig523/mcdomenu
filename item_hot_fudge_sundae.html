What's in a Hot Fudge Sundae?

McDonald's vanilla soft-serve ice cream in a plastic cup topped with warm, thick hot fudge chocolate sauce. The temperature contrast between the warm sauce and cold ice cream is what makes the sundae work. There are no additional toppings like nuts or whipped cream — just ice cream and hot fudge. Simple and satisfying.

Hot Fudge Sundae vs Other Desserts

The Hot Fudge Sundae is the sweet spot on the dessert menu — chocolate flavor at a moderate price. It costs $2 less than an Oreo McFlurry and has 180 fewer calories. The Hot Caramel Sundae is the same price with 10 more calories — the only difference is caramel sauce versus hot fudge. Choose based on whether you prefer chocolate or caramel.

Ice Cream Machine and Deals

Like all ice cream items, the sundae depends on the soft-serve machine. Check the McDonald's app before making a special trip — it shows ice cream availability at your nearest location. Best chance is during regular daytime hours between 11 AM and 9 PM. The McDonald's app occasionally offers dessert promotions. Pair with a Baked Apple Pie ($1.89) for a warm-and-cold combo under $5.
