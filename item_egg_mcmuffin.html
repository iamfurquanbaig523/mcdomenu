What's in an Egg McMuffin?

A round freshly-cracked USDA Grade A egg, a slice of Canadian bacon (lean ham), and a slice of American cheese on a toasted English muffin. The egg is cooked in a ring mold to get the perfect round shape that fits the muffin. Unlike most McDonald's breakfast sandwiches that use scrambled egg mix or folded eggs, the Egg McMuffin uses a whole cracked egg — which is why it tastes fresher. The Canadian bacon is lean pork loin, not a sausage patty, making the sandwich lighter overall.

Nutrition Facts

Egg McMuffin vs Other Breakfast Sandwiches

The Egg McMuffin has the best calorie-to-protein ratio of any breakfast sandwich — 310 calories for 17g protein versus the Sausage McGriddles at 550 calories for just 3 more grams of protein. If you want something cheaper, the Sausage McMuffin at $2.29 saves you $2.20 but has 90 more calories and double the fat.

Egg McMuffin Meal and Deals

The Egg McMuffin Meal includes hash browns and a small coffee for about $6.49-$6.99. But the better deal is the $4 Breakfast Meal Deal from the McValue menu — a breakfast sandwich, hash brown, and small coffee for $4 flat. The Egg McMuffin also appears regularly in McDonald's app promotions. MyMcDonald's Rewards lets you earn points toward a free Egg McMuffin.

Availability

The Egg McMuffin is available during breakfast hours only — typically until 10:30 AM on weekdays and 11:00 AM on weekends. It is not on the all-day menu. The Egg McMuffin was invented by Herb Peterson in 1971 and launched nationwide in 1975, making it McDonald's first-ever breakfast sandwich and the item that created the entire fast food breakfast category.
