What You Get

Forty Chicken McNuggets made from white-meat chicken — breast, rib, and tenderloin — in four shapes (bell, ball, boot, and bone). Each nugget is seasoned, battered, and fried until golden. You get 6 dipping sauces included. Available sauces include Tangy BBQ, Sweet 'N Sour, Honey Mustard, Spicy Buffalo, and Creamy Ranch.

McNuggets Size Comparison

The 40-Piece saves 42% per nugget compared to buying four 10-piece boxes ($21.96 vs $12.99). That is a $8.97 savings. Even versus two 20-piece boxes ($17.98), the 40-piece saves $4.99. For groups of 3-4, it is the clear choice.

Tips

McNuggets reheat well in an air fryer at 375°F for 3-4 minutes — so ordering a 40-piece for meal prep works. The McDonald's app occasionally offers the 40-piece at a discount. Check the McValue menu for bundle deals.
