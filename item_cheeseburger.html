A McDonald's Cheeseburger costs $1.89 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.49 in Mississippi to $2.99 in Hawaii. It has 300 calories — one of the most affordable items on the entire menu and a best-seller since the 1960s.

What's in a McDonald's Cheeseburger?

A single 100% beef patty (1.6 oz before cooking) with a slice of melted American cheese, pickles, diced onions, ketchup, and mustard on a lightly toasted regular bun. No lettuce, no tomato, no special sauce — just the classic beef-and-cheese combination McDonald's has used for decades. The cheese is placed on the patty right off the grill so it melts slightly.

Cheeseburger vs McDouble vs Double Cheeseburger

The Cheeseburger is the cheapest burger with cheese at $1.89. For $1 more, the McDouble doubles the beef — the best value upgrade on the menu. The Double Cheeseburger at $3.49 adds a second cheese slice. If cheese is not important, the Hamburger at $2.49 saves $0.30 but oddly costs more at some locations.

Deals and Budget Tips

The Cheeseburger is on the McValue menu and appears in Buy 1 Add 1 for $1 promotions — two for about $2.89. It is also part of the $5 Meal Deal with a 4-piece McNuggets, small fries, and small drink. The McDonald's app runs daily deals that frequently include the Cheeseburger.
