A McChicken costs $2.29 on average in the US in 2026. Prices range from $1.79 in Mississippi to $3.49 in Hawaii. It has 400 calories and is the cheapest chicken sandwich at McDonald's — a budget favorite that has been on the menu since the 1980s.

What's in a McChicken?

A breaded chicken patty made from a blend of white and dark meat chicken, shredded lettuce, and mayonnaise on a plain toasted bun. The patty is lightly seasoned with a tempura-style breading that gives it a mild crunch. There are no pickles, no cheese, and no fancy sauce — just chicken, lettuce, and mayo. The simplicity is part of the appeal: it is a straightforward chicken sandwich for a low price.

Nutrition Facts

The McChicken has the lowest sodium of any sandwich on the menu at just 560mg — less than half the sodium of a Big Mac (1050mg). Allergens include wheat, milk, egg, and soy. See our allergen guide.

McChicken vs Other Chicken Options

The McChicken costs less than half the price of every other chicken sandwich. The trade-off is the chicken quality — the McChicken uses a blended chicken patty while the McCrispy uses a whole chicken breast fillet. The McCrispy is a bigger, crunchier, higher-quality sandwich. But if you are eating on a budget, the McChicken gives you 400 calories and a decent sandwich for $2.29.

McChicken Meal Deal

The McChicken is one of the sandwich options in the McChicken Meal Deal — a McChicken, 4-piece McNuggets, small fries, and a small drink for about $5.99. That is about 900 calories of food for $6. The McChicken is also part of the McValue Under $3 Menu and the Buy 1 Add 1 for $1 lunch deal, where you can add a second McChicken for just $1.

Popular Customizations

The most popular McChicken customization is adding a slice of cheese ($0.30) — sometimes called a "McChicken with Cheese." You can also add bacon ($1.50), swap mayo for spicy buffalo or ranch sauce, or order it with no mayo and add your own sauce from the sauces menu. Some customers order a McChicken and a McDouble together, stack them, and call it a "McGangBang" — an unofficial menu hack that gives you both chicken and beef for about $5.
