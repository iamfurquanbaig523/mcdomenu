McCrispy Lineup: Prices, Heat Levels, and What's Different

The McCrispy is the flagship chicken sandwich on the McDonald's chicken and fish menu USA. It replaced the Crispy Chicken Sandwich as the premium crispy option and now anchors the category with four distinct variants across two price tiers. Understanding what separates each one saves you from ordering the wrong sandwich.

McCrispy vs. Spicy McCrispy

Both the standard and spicy versions are priced identically at $4.99. The only difference is the seasoning on the chicken patty â€” the Spicy McCrispy uses a heat-forward breading that adds 60 calories over the standard version.

Key takeaway: At the same price, the choice between McCrispy and Spicy McCrispy is purely about heat preference. If you're calorie-conscious, the standard McCrispy saves you 60 kcal for zero extra cost.

Deluxe McCrispy vs. Spicy Deluxe McCrispy

The Deluxe variants step up to $6.49 and add a more substantial topping set: tomato, lettuce, and mayo on top of the same crispy chicken base. The Spicy Deluxe adds the heat-forward patty on top of those toppings, landing at 570 calories.

Is the Deluxe Upgrade Worth $1.50?

The $1.50 jump from McCrispy to Deluxe McCrispy buys you tomato, upgraded lettuce, and mayo. Whether that's worth it depends on what you're building. If you're ordering a standalone sandwich and want something that feels more complete, the Deluxe is a reasonable upgrade. If you're inside an Extra Value Meal and already have fries and a drink, the standard McCrispy does the job at a lower total.

McDonald's Chicken and Fish Menu: Frequently Asked Questions

What is on the McDonald's chicken and fish menu?

The McDonald's chicken and fish menu USA currently tracks the McChicken, McCrispy, Spicy McCrispy, Deluxe McCrispy, Spicy Deluxe McCrispy, Filet-O-Fish, and McCrispy Strips 3-pc. McNuggets are covered separately on the McNuggets and Strips page.

How much is the McChicken at McDonald's?

The McChicken is $3.59 on the tracked USA menu, making it the cheapest chicken sandwich in the chicken and fish category. It also appears in McValue bundle deals that can reduce the effective cost further.

What is the difference between McCrispy and Deluxe McCrispy?

The standard McCrispy comes with pickles and butter on a potato roll. The Deluxe McCrispy adds tomato, lettuce, and mayo for $1.50 more ($6.49 vs. $4.99). Both use the same crispy chicken patty. The Deluxe feels more like a complete sandwich; the standard is a cleaner, simpler build.

How many calories are in the Filet-O-Fish?

The Filet-O-Fish has 390 calories on the tracked USA menu. It's the lowest-calorie sandwich in the $5+ price tier, lighter than both the McCrispy (470 kcal) and the Deluxe McCrispy (510 kcal). It's made with wild-caught Alaskan Pollock, tartar sauce, American cheese, and a steamed bun.

Is the Spicy McCrispy spicier than the McCrispy?

Yes. The Spicy McCrispy uses a heat-forward seasoned breading compared to the standard McCrispy's milder coating. The heat level is noticeable but not extreme â€” it's a medium-heat sandwich by fast food standards. The Spicy Deluxe McCrispy has the same spicy patty with additional toppings.

What is the healthiest item on the McDonald's chicken and fish menu?

The McCrispy Strips 3-pc (~380 kcal) and Filet-O-Fish (390 kcal) are the lowest-calorie options in this category. If you're prioritizing calories, both come in under 400 kcal. The nutrition and allergen guide has full sodium, fat, and allergen data for each item.

Does McDonald's chicken and fish menu include nuggets?

No. McNuggets are tracked separately on the McNuggets and Strips page, which covers 4, 6, 10, 20, and 40-piece options. The chicken and fish menu focuses on sandwiches and the 3-pc McCrispy Strips.

Are McDonald's chicken sandwich prices the same in every state?

No. Prices vary by franchise operator, state and local taxes, urban vs. rural cost structures, and app participation. The prices on this page are based on currently tracked USA menu data. See the prices by state guide for regional context.

No. The Happy Meal uses McNuggets (4-pc or 6-pc) or a Hamburger as the protein option, not the sandwiches from this category. The Happy Meal McNugget options are covered on the Happy Meal page.
