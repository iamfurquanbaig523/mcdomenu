The McDonald’s Grinch Meal is a limited-time holiday offering built around classic McDonald’s items with a seasonal twist: a choice of Big Mac or 10-piece Chicken McNuggets, a medium drink, Dill Pickle "Grinch Salt" McShaker Fries, and collectible Grinch socks at participating U.S. restaurants.

This page covers what is included, how the McShaker fries work, how pricing can vary by location, and where the Grinch Meal fits inside the wider McDonald’s limited-time menu.

Quick answer: The McDonald’s Grinch Meal is best understood as a holiday limited-time meal, not a permanent menu category. It combines a familiar entree, fries, drink, pickle-seasoning experience, and collectible socks, so readers should compare it with both the limited-time menu and regular combo meals before ordering.

What Is the McDonald’s Grinch Meal?

The McDonald’s Grinch Meal is a special holiday bundle created around the Grinch character and McDonald’s McShaker Fries format. Instead of being just another burger or nugget meal, it adds a hands-on seasoning step and a collectible holiday item, which is why search interest is stronger than a normal combo meal page.

The meal usually gives customers a choice between a Big Mac or 10-piece Chicken McNuggets, plus medium fries, a medium drink, Dill Pickle "Grinch Salt" seasoning, and Grinch-themed socks where available.

The McShaker Fries Experience

The standout feature is the Dill Pickle "Grinch Salt" McShaker Fries. Instead of eating regular McDonald’s fries straight from the carton, customers add the seasoning packet to the fries bag, close it, and shake until the fries are coated.

That interactive step is the main differentiator. It gives the meal a stronger novelty angle than a standard combo, while the dill pickle flavor connects the food directly to the Grinch theme: tart, tangy, and intentionally mischievous.

What Comes With the Grinch Meal?

  • Choice of entree: Big Mac or 10-piece Chicken McNuggets, depending on location and app availability.
  • Dill Pickle McShaker Fries: Fries served with a Grinch Salt seasoning packet and a shake bag.
  • Medium drink: Usually selected from the regular McDonald’s drinks menu.
  • Collectible Grinch socks: A holiday merchandise item included while supplies last at participating restaurants.

McDonald’s Grinch Meal Price

The McDonald’s Grinch Meal price varies by location because U.S. McDonald’s restaurants can price menu items differently by market, franchise, app channel, and delivery platform. Some high-cost markets may show a noticeably higher price than suburban or lower-cost regions.

The safest way to confirm the current Grinch Meal price is to open the McDonald’s app, choose your local restaurant, and compare the offer against regular McDonald’s meal deals. That matters because a limited-time bundle can feel exciting, but the best value still depends on the final checkout price.

Where Is the Grinch Meal Available?

The Grinch Meal is a participating-location limited-time offer. That means it may be visible nationally in marketing, but actual availability can still depend on restaurant participation, inventory, app setup, and whether the socks or seasoning packets are still in stock.

Readers should check availability through the McDonald’s app, at the drive-thru, or with the local restaurant before making a trip specifically for the meal. Delivery platforms may also show different availability and pricing than in-store ordering.

Why the Grinch Meal Fits the Limited-Time Menu

The Grinch Meal belongs in limited-time menu coverage because its value is tied to seasonality, novelty, collectible socks, and McShaker Fries rather than a permanent menu role. It is closer to a holiday promotion than a standard Big Mac Meal or McNuggets Meal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the McDonald’s Grinch Meal permanent?

No. It is a limited-time holiday meal, so availability depends on the promotion window and local inventory.

What are Grinch Salt McShaker Fries?

They are McDonald’s fries served with a dill pickle seasoning packet and a shake bag so customers can coat the fries themselves.

Can I get the Grinch Meal with Big Mac or McNuggets?

Participating restaurants commonly position the meal around a choice of Big Mac or 10-piece Chicken McNuggets, but exact availability should be checked in the app.

Does every Grinch Meal include socks?

The socks are part of the holiday promotion while supplies last, but availability can run out by restaurant.

Is the McDonald’s Grinch Meal Worth Trying?

The Grinch Meal is worth considering if you want a seasonal McDonald’s experience rather than the lowest possible menu price. The strongest reasons to try it are the Dill Pickle McShaker Fries, the collectible socks, and the holiday tie-in. If value is the main goal, compare it with standard combo meals and app deals before ordering.
