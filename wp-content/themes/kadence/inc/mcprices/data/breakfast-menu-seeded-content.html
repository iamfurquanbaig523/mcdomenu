McDonald's Breakfast Menu Hours: When Is Breakfast Served?

Breakfast is the most time-sensitive part of McDonald's ordering. Most U.S. restaurants begin breakfast around 5:00 AM, with many weekday breakfast windows ending around 10:30 AM. Saturday and Sunday service often runs to 11:00 AM, especially at participating locations, but the final answer is always local.

The safest ordering habit is simple: check the McDonald's app before leaving, then order earlier than the cutoff. A full meal with a sandwich, Hash Browns, and coffee is easier to place through Mobile Order & Pay while the breakfast menu is still active.

Can You Get McDonald's All-Day Breakfast?

No. McDonald's all-day breakfast is not available as a standard nationwide U.S. program in 2026. A small number of restaurants may test or keep limited items locally, but most locations sell breakfast only during the morning service window.

Treat all-day breakfast as a local exception, not a promise. If a breakfast craving hits later in the day, use the full McDonald's menu hub or the McDonald's deals and McValue guide to compare regular menu value options instead.

What's Included on the McDonald's Breakfast Menu

The breakfast menu is easiest to understand by format. McMuffins use a toasted English muffin and tend to feel lighter. Biscuits are richer and more filling. McGriddles use sweet maple-flavored griddle cakes. Bagels are available at select locations and usually sit at the heavier end of the sandwich lineup. Platters, Hotcakes, Hash Browns, Sausage Burrito, and oatmeal round out the non-sandwich choices.

  • McMuffins: classic English muffin sandwiches led by the Egg McMuffin.
  • Biscuits: buttery biscuit builds such as Sausage Biscuit and Bacon, Egg & Cheese Biscuit.
  • McGriddles: sweet-savory breakfast sandwiches, including Sausage, Egg & Cheese McGriddles.
  • Bagels: select-location sandwiches, with Steak, Egg & Cheese Bagel as the premium option.
  • Platters and sides: Big Breakfast with Hotcakes, Hotcakes, Hash Browns, Sausage Burrito, and Fruit & Maple Oatmeal.

Standout & Best Items on the McDonald's Breakfast Menu

The best breakfast order depends on whether the morning priority is calories, cost, speed, or fullness. These are the items that deserve special attention because they answer different real ordering intents.

Egg McMuffin - The All-Time Classic

The Egg McMuffin is still the benchmark fast-food breakfast sandwich. It is compact, easy to eat on a commute, and lower in calories than heavier sausage, biscuit, or bagel builds. It also gives the page a clear comparison anchor for every other breakfast sandwich.

Sausage Burrito - Quick Value Pick

The Sausage Burrito is the fastest handheld option on the breakfast menu. It works well for commuters because it is small, warm, and easy to pair with coffee or Hash Browns without creating a large tray order.

McGriddles - Sweet and Savory Differentiator

McGriddles are unique because the sweet maple-style griddle cakes act like the bun. That preparation style is the differentiator: it creates a sweet-savory bite that standard biscuits, bagels, and McMuffins do not copy.

Big Breakfast with Hotcakes - Full Morning Meal

The Big Breakfast with Hotcakes is the most complete breakfast plate in the lineup and the heaviest calorie decision on this page. It is best treated as a full meal, not a small add-on to coffee.

Fruit & Maple Oatmeal - Lighter Comfort Option

Fruit & Maple Oatmeal gives the breakfast menu a softer, lighter lane for readers who do not want a sandwich. It is also one of the easiest items to compare against coffee when the goal is a simple breakfast that does not feel too heavy.

Frequently Asked Questions About McDonald's Breakfast

What time does McDonald's stop serving breakfast?

Most U.S. locations stop breakfast around 10:30 AM on weekdays and around 11:00 AM on weekends, but hours vary by restaurant. Use the breakfast hours guide and the app for local confirmation.

Does McDonald's still offer all-day breakfast?

No. All-day breakfast is not a standard nationwide program in 2026. Some restaurants may have local exceptions, but most locations only sell breakfast during morning hours.

What is the cheapest item on the McDonald's breakfast menu?

The lower-cost breakfast picks are usually Sausage Burrito, Sausage McMuffin, Sausage Biscuit, and Hash Browns. Local prices and app deals can change the winner.

What is the healthiest McDonald's breakfast item?

Fruit & Maple Oatmeal is usually the lightest comfort option by sodium and overall feel. For a sandwich, the Egg McMuffin is the classic lighter benchmark. Always verify ingredients and allergens officially.

How many calories are in a McDonald's breakfast?

It depends on the order. A simple item like Hash Browns is around 150 calories, while the Big Breakfast with Hotcakes reaches about 1,340 calories before drink changes or customizations.

Can I order McDonald's breakfast through delivery?

Yes, breakfast delivery is available at participating locations during breakfast hours through McDelivery and third-party platforms. Delivery prices and fees are usually higher than pickup.

What comes in a McDonald's breakfast combo meal?

A breakfast combo usually includes a breakfast sandwich, Hash Browns, and a drink such as coffee or another beverage. Exact drink options and prices can vary by location.

Are McDonald's Hash Browns gluten-free?

No. Do not treat Hash Browns as gluten-free. Use official allergen information and ask the restaurant if gluten sensitivity or celiac disease matters for your order.

Which McDonald's breakfast item has the highest protein?

Steak and sausage bagel builds and larger sausage-egg combinations usually sit near the top for protein. The Steak, Egg & Cheese Bagel is one of the stronger protein-focused sandwich choices where available.

Is the Sausage Burrito a quick breakfast option?

Yes. The Sausage Burrito is one of the quickest handheld breakfast choices and works well when you want something warmer than a side but smaller than a full sandwich combo.

What is the most popular McDonald's breakfast item?

The Egg McMuffin is the best-known McDonald's breakfast item and remains the benchmark for the entire McMuffin lineup.

Do McDonald's breakfast prices vary by state or location?

Yes. Prices can vary by state, city, franchise operator, taxes, app participation, and delivery method. Use the prices by state guide for broader context.

What does the McDonald's breakfast menu include?

It includes McMuffins, biscuits, McGriddles, select-location bagels, Hotcakes, Big Breakfast plates, Hash Browns, Sausage Burrito, oatmeal, coffee pairings, and breakfast combo meals.

Which breakfast order works for a relaxed weekend meal?

The Big Breakfast with Hotcakes, Hotcakes and Sausage, or a bagel combo works better for slower weekend mornings than a small commuter-style sandwich.
