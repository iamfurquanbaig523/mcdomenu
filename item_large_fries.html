Nutrition Facts

Fries Size Comparison

Large Fries are the best value per ounce at $0.54 — 25% cheaper per ounce than Medium. For just $0.40 more than Medium, you get 2 more ounces of fries. The trade-off is 170 extra calories. Large makes sense when sharing, when fries are the main event, or when you want the most food for your money.

Deals and Tips

The McDonald's app frequently runs free Large Fries promotions — often on Fridays with any purchase. Large Fries pair well with lighter sandwiches like the Hamburger ($2.49) or McChicken ($2.29). Dip them in Creamy Ranch or a Vanilla Cone for the classic sweet-and-salty combo.
