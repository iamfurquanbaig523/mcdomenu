McDonald’s UK Menu Prices 2026 — Everything You Need to Know

The McDonald’s UK menu has long been a benchmark for fast food affordability, offering everything from a £1.19 Hamburger to the premium £8.79 Big Arch Meal. Despite price fluctuations due to inflation and ingredient costs, McDonald’s UK continues to balance value, taste, and convenience — a formula that has secured its dominance in the Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) market.

In 2026, the average McDonald’s meal price in the UK increased by 6–8%, following the national rise in food and energy costs. Prices vary depending on location, franchise ownership, and regional VAT rates. For example: London outlets often charge £0.30–£0.60 more per item than regional branches. Manchester and Birmingham prices are typically aligned with national averages.

The McDonald’s UK App automatically adjusts to local pricing, allowing customers to view real-time prices before ordering. McDonald’s breakfast hours in the UK run from 5:00 AM to 11:00 AM. The Big Breakfast Wrap and Bacon Roll Meal remain among the UK’s most popular morning combos.

McDelivery, available via the McDonald’s App, Uber Eats, and Just Eat, has expanded to 90% of UK locations. Delivery charges average £2.99–£4.99, depending on distance. App orders may feature exclusive deals, such as 20% off your first McDelivery order.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about McDonald’s UK menu and prices.

How much is a Big Mac in the UK in 2026? +
A Big Mac on its own costs £5.29 in 2026. As a meal (with medium fries and a medium drink), it costs £7.69. Prices may vary slightly depending on location — London restaurants can be 30–60p more expensive than the national average.
What’s new on McDonald’s UK menu in 2026? +
April 2026 brings the Big Arch, Double Big Mac, Cadbury Creme Egg McFlurry, Double Big Tasty, and Cheesy Chicken Stack — all limited time. The Sausage & Egg McGriddles have also returned for breakfast. Check our “What’s New” section above for full details.
How often are McDonald’s UK prices updated? +
We review and update prices monthly. Our last full update was April 2026. If you spot an error, please use our contact page to let us know.
How can I get cheaper McDonald’s in the UK? +
The best way is to use the McDonald’s app, which offers regular exclusive deals — sometimes up to 70% off. MyMcDonald’s Rewards also lets you earn points for free food. The Saver Menu has items starting from £1.19.
Do McDonald’s prices vary by location in the UK? +
Yes, prices can vary — typically by 10–20p — depending on whether the restaurant is at an airport, motorway service station, or a standard location. Central London restaurants also tend to be slightly more expensive.
What time does McDonald’s breakfast end in the UK? +
McDonald’s UK breakfast is typically served from 5:00 AM to 11:00 AM. After 11am, the full daytime menu becomes available. Some 24-hour locations may have slightly different schedules — check the McDonald’s app for your nearest restaurant.
What are the cheapest items on the McDonald’s UK menu? +
The cheapest items are from the Saver Menu: Hamburger (£1.19), Cheeseburger (£1.39), Mayo Chicken (£1.39), Side Salad (£1.19), Pineapple Stick (£0.95), and Carrot Bag (£0.95). A hot coffee (White Coffee) is also just £0.99.
Is there a vegan option at McDonald’s UK? +
Yes — the McPlant (£4.79) made with a Beyond Meat patty is fully vegan when ordered without cheese. The Spicy Veggie One wrap, Veggie Dippers, Side Salad, Fries, and Carrot Bag are also vegan-friendly.
What is the McDonald’s World Menu Heist 2026? +
The World Menu Heist is a limited-time 2026 promotion featuring globally inspired items from international McDonald’s menus. Items include the Pineapple McSpicy from Australia, Mac & Cheese Triangles from the UAE, Pepper McNuggets from Japan, McShaker Fries from Poland, Pretzel McFlurry from Malaysia, and Choco Caramel Pie from Indonesia.
How does MyMcDonald’s Rewards work? +
MyMcDonald’s Rewards lets you earn 100 points for every £1 spent via the app. Redeem points for free food and drinks — coffees, burgers, McFlurries, and more. Frequent app users save an estimated £8–£12 per month. Points are earned on all app orders including McDelivery and Click & Collect.
Does McDonald’s UK do home delivery? +
Yes — McDelivery is available at 90% of UK McDonald’s locations. Order via the McDonald’s App, Uber Eats, Just Eat, or Deliveroo. Average delivery time is 20–35 minutes. Delivery charges range from £2.99–£4.99 depending on distance and platform.
Is McDonald’s beef 100% British? +
Yes — all McDonald’s UK beef burgers use 100% British and Irish beef with no fillers or additives. McDonald’s has maintained this commitment since 2002, sourcing from trusted British and Irish farms meeting strict quality and welfare standards.
Are McDonald’s prices higher at airports and motorway services? +
Yes — McDonald’s at airports, motorway services, and train stations typically charge £0.50–£1.00 more per item due to higher operating costs. Standard restaurant prices are consistent across most high-street and retail-park locations in the UK.
What allergens are in McDonald’s UK food? +
Most McDonald’s menu items contain common allergens including wheat, soya, egg, milk, sesame, and mustard. Full allergen information is available on the McDonald’s UK App, official website, and in-restaurant information boards. Always inform staff if you have a severe allergy before ordering.

McDonald’s UK History & Locations

From a single restaurant in Woolwich in 1974 to 1,481 locations across Britain today.

1974
First UK Restaurant Opens
McDonald’s opened its first UK restaurant in Woolwich, South East London — beginning a 50-year journey across Britain.
1983
Chicken McNuggets Launch
McNuggets launched nationwide in 1983, becoming one of McDonald’s most beloved menu items across all age groups.
1998
McCafé Launched in the UK
McCafé expanded McDonald’s into the coffee shop market, offering freshly ground Arabica coffee as an affordable alternative to high-street chains.
2007
Calorie Labelling Introduced
McDonald’s UK was one of the first major fast-food chains to display calorie information on packaging and in-store menus.
2021
McPlant Launches Nationwide
The McPlant, developed with Beyond Meat, launched across the UK — becoming McDonald’s first dedicated vegan burger option.
2026
World Menu Heist & Big Arch
April 2026 saw the launch of the Big Arch, World Menu Heist international items, Spicy McNuggets, and the Meal Deal Plus bundle.
